How the radar prices a launch

Priced on every run from the snapshot's own figures — no hardcoded names or values. Four pricing forms are fitted on the protocols that already trade (global median multiple, category median multiple shrunk k=5, log-log curve, curve + shrunk category intercept); leave-one-out picks the winner on this snapshot. The base price is then moved by seven-day TVL growth (×0.5, clamped −60%…+120%), by how long the protocol has run with no token (maturity 0.6…1.0 over 1095 days) and by chain breadth (+3% per extra chain, capped at 4). Rank follows implied value; launch odds are the empirical token rate of dated protocols in the same age bucket, shrunk by category, and expected value = implied value × launch odds.

Movers — fastest TVL growth, last 7 days

Growth is one of the three factors that move the implied value, so the fastest movers are where the board can change fastest.

Validation — is the pricing any good?

Leave-one-out: every trading protocol is priced once with its own market cap hidden, and the median absolute log error is reported per form. The out-of-sample check fits the comps curve on the oldest half of traders and tests it on the newest half.
